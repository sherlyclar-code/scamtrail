      Get help from official services

      These services are free. They will never ask you to pay to get your money back.

      • Scamwatch

        Report a scam and see the latest scam alerts.

        scamwatch.gov.au (opens in a new tab)
      • ReportCyber

        Report online crime, hacking or money lost online.

        cyber.gov.au (opens in a new tab)
      • IDCARE

        Free help if your identity or personal details were stolen.

        1800 595 160
        idcare.org (opens in a new tab)
      • AFCA

        Free, independent complaints if your bank won't help.

        1800 931 678
        afca.org.au (opens in a new tab)
      • Your bank

        Call straight away if you've paid or shared details.

        Use the number on the back of your card or in your bank's official app.
